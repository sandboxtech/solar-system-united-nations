## 中文版

```text
[img=space-location/solar-system-edge] [color=acid][font=default-large-bold]太阳系联合国 · United Nations of Sol[/font][/color]
[color=acid]欢迎 · 歡迎 · Welcome · Willkommen · Bienvenido · Bienvenue · Bem-vindo · ようこそ · 환영합니다 · Witamy · Добро пожаловать[/color]

[font=default-bold]你守护的不是一张永远不变的地图，而是一套能穿过星球跃迁的产业。[/font]

五个国家在同一个太阳系里开拓、生产、交易和远征。
你可以专心建设，也可以成为地产商、市场玩家、外交官或入侵者。

[img=virtual-signal/signal-map-marker] [color=yellow][font=default-bold]跃迁玩法[/font][/color]

五颗公共星球分别计算跃迁倒计时。
每次抵达的都是一颗拥有全新地形、资源分布和星球特色的新世界。

公共星球上的旧基地不会同行，但本国联合国难民营和房产会留下。
提前转移关键设备、稀有物资和预制产线，下一轮就能更快站稳脚跟。

[img=item/construction-robot] [color=yellow][font=default-bold]厂房玩法[/font][/color]

生产科技瓶，积累个人经验，再用经验建造自己的厂房。

[img=item/automation-science-pack] 普通小屋、[img=item/logistic-science-pack] 市场小屋、[img=item/military-science-pack] 安全小屋，以及铁路、网格、太空、熔岩、油海、农园和冰原等主题厂房，都有不同的空间、环境或功能。

选择建造等级，游戏会直接预览面积、寿命、降价周期和成本。
厂房可以续期、扩建或回收，你决定它是一处临时据点，还是长期经营的产业基地。

[img=item/coin] [color=yellow][font=default-bold]地产玩法[/font][/color]

建造自己的房产，也可以购买其他玩家出售的本国房产。
地球还提供多种国有样板房，让新居民直接选择喜欢的厂房原型。

房价会随时间回落，成交后重新上涨；原屋主会收到扣税后的成交款。
经营、换手、加价和寻找低价房，让每套房产都成为玩家经济的一部分。

外国房产可以查看，但本国居民才拥有当地的购买、进入和避难权利。

[img=entity/market] [color=yellow][font=default-bold]市场玩法[/font][/color]

五个国家拥有彼此独立的本地市场。
直接用背包买卖原料、零件和科技瓶，价格会随真实库存和交易方向变化。

市场小屋还能按你在铁箱中设定的目标库存，自动买卖钢箱里的货物。
市场不是固定价格商店；每一次大宗交易，都会改变后来者看到的行情。

[img=space-location/solar-system-edge] [color=yellow][font=default-bold]阵营玩法[/font][/color]

[img=space-location/nauvis] 地球人民共和国
[img=space-location/vulcanus] 火星远征军政府
[img=space-location/fulgora] 雷星联合财团
[img=space-location/gleba] 草星自由公社
[img=space-location/aquilo] 冰星技术官僚学院

各国拥有独立科技、市场、资产、房产和太空平台。
国家之间可能开放边界、共享视野并停火，也可能转为敌对。

冰星长期保持中立；其他国家的外交关系会随着发展和跃迁而变化。
你可以留在故土建设，也可以远征外国，甚至亲自抵达对方星球后叛国加入。

[img=item/linked-chest] [color=yellow][font=default-bold]关联箱玩法[/font][/color]

玩家亲手合成 [img=item/wooden-chest] 木箱，会得到个人关联箱。
关联箱不能直接打开，需要使用机械臂存取。

把科技瓶送进去，它会转化为你的个人经验。
房产自带关联物流，与屋主的个人关联库存相连，可以保存物资和预制工厂。

公共星球和难民营都有传送门与国家关联箱，既能来回通行，也能撤运物资。

[img=virtual-signal/signal-info] [color=acid][font=default-bold]第一轮先做什么[/font][/color]

① 从难民营的传送门前往本国公共星球，抵达后先看左上角的跃迁倒计时。
② 像普通游戏一样采矿、供电和建设工厂，但给撤运留下时间和物流出口。
③ 跃迁前，把关键设备和物资带回难民营，或送进国家关联箱；新世界抵达后，再带着它们重新开拓。

先经历一次完整跃迁，就已经掌握了这个场景的核心。
科技瓶经验、厂房、地产和市场，可以在站稳脚跟后慢慢了解。

[font=default-bold]无需第三方 MOD，需要 Space Age。服务器当前运行 Factorio 2.1 测试版。[/font]
```

## English version

```text
[img=space-location/solar-system-edge] [color=acid][font=default-large-bold]United Nations of Sol · 太阳系联合国[/font][/color]
[color=acid]Welcome · 欢迎 · 歡迎 · Willkommen · Bienvenido · Bienvenue · Bem-vindo · ようこそ · 환영합니다 · Witamy · Добро пожаловать[/color]

[font=default-bold]You are not defending one permanent map. You are building an industry that can survive from world to world.[/font]

Five nations explore, manufacture, trade, and compete in one shared solar system.
Build in peace, speculate on property, move markets, negotiate alliances, or become an invader.

[img=virtual-signal/signal-map-marker] [color=yellow][font=default-bold]Planetary Shifts[/font][/color]

Each of the five public planets has its own shift countdown.
Every arrival brings a new world with different terrain, resources, and planetary traits.

Factories left on the public planet do not travel with it, but your national UN Refugee Camp and property remain.
Move valuable machines, rare materials, and prefab production lines before departure, then rebuild faster in the next world.

[img=item/construction-robot] [color=yellow][font=default-bold]Factory Interiors[/font][/color]

Produce science packs, earn personal experience, and spend it to construct your own factory interior.

[img=item/automation-science-pack] starter workshops, [img=item/logistic-science-pack] market workshops, [img=item/military-science-pack] fortified bunkers, and specialized railway, grid, orbital, lava, oil, plantation, and cryogenic layouts each offer distinct space, environments, or functions.

Choose a construction level and preview the dimensions, lifetime, price-decay period, and cost before building.
Renew, expand, or salvage an interior as your plans change.

[img=item/coin] [color=yellow][font=default-bold]Real Estate[/font][/color]

Construct your own property or purchase one offered by another citizen of your nation.
Nauvis also offers a range of national model homes, letting new residents choose a ready-made factory archetype.

Asking prices fall over time and rise after a sale; the previous owner receives the proceeds after tax.
Build, trade, and raise prices until each property becomes part of a player-driven economy.

You may inspect foreign property, but local citizens hold the right to buy, enter, and shelter there.

[img=entity/market] [color=yellow][font=default-bold]National Markets[/font][/color]

Every nation operates an independent local market.
Trade raw materials, components, and science packs directly from your inventory. Prices respond to real stock and the direction of trade.

A Market Workshop can automatically buy and sell goods from its steel chest to approach the target stock placed in its iron chest.
This is not a fixed-price shop: every large trade changes the market seen by the next player.

[img=space-location/solar-system-edge] [color=yellow][font=default-bold]Five Factions[/font][/color]

[img=space-location/nauvis] People's Republic of Nauvis
[img=space-location/vulcanus] Vulcanus Expeditionary Command
[img=space-location/fulgora] Fulgoran Combine
[img=space-location/gleba] Gleban Free Commune
[img=space-location/aquilo] Aquilo Technocratic Academy

Each nation has separate research, markets, assets, property, and space platforms.
Countries may open borders, share explored charts, and observe a ceasefire—or become hostile.

Aquilo remains neutral, while relations among the other nations evolve with progress and planetary shifts.
Develop your homeland, launch foreign expeditions, or reach another nation's planet and defect to it.

[img=item/linked-chest] [color=yellow][font=default-bold]Linked Logistics[/font][/color]

Hand-craft an [img=item/wooden-chest] wooden chest to receive a personal linked chest.
It cannot be opened directly; inserters move items in and out.

Science packs deposited inside become personal experience.
Property includes linked logistics that share your personal linked inventory, allowing supplies and prefab factories to survive outside the shifting public world.

The public planet and Refugee Camp have portals and national linked chests for travel and evacuation.

[img=virtual-signal/signal-info] [color=acid][font=default-bold]Your first worldshift[/font][/color]

1. Take the Refugee Camp portal to your public home planet. On arrival, check the shift countdown in the upper-left corner.
2. Mine, generate power, and build a normal factory, but leave enough time and logistics capacity for evacuation.
3. Before the shift, carry essential machines and materials back to camp or send them through national linked chests. Bring them out again when the new world arrives.

Once you have lived through one complete shift, you understand the scenario's core loop.
Science experience, factory interiors, real estate, and markets can wait until your first foothold is secure.

[font=default-bold]No third-party mods. Space Age is required. The server currently runs the Factorio 2.1 experimental branch.[/font]
```
